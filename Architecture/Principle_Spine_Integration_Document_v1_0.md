### **P2 — Coordination**

### **P4 — Resilience**  
Stability, stress‑safe reasoning, reflective pacing, durability.

These four principles form the **governance spine**.

### **4.1 Sequential Integration Rule**

\[
G = f(P1, P2, P3, P4)

- \(P1\) = Clarity  
- \(P2\) = Coordination  
- \(P3\) = Alignment  
- \(P4\) = Resilience

### **4.2 Integration Constraints**

- Principles must integrate **in order**.  
- No principle may override another.  
- No principle may form hierarchy.  
- No principle may drift into metaphor.  
- No principle may collapse into authority.  

Formal invariant:

\[
P_i \not\rightarrow \text{authority}
\]

### **4.3 Integration Flow**

```
Clarity → Coordination → Alignment → Resilience
```

Clarity establishes language.  
Coordination establishes interaction.  
Alignment establishes coherence.  
Resilience establishes durability.

This is the **governance backbone**.

# 📘 **Governance Practice Layer Specification (v1.0)**  
### *Charters • Protocols • Decision Flows • Communication Norms • Stability Practices*

---

## ⭐ **1 — Identity**  
**Name:** Governance Practice Layer  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Domain:** Principle‑Based Governance Frameworks  
**Role:**  
Translate the unified principle spine into actionable governance artifacts while preserving clarity, non‑hierarchy, stability, and mode purity.

This is the layer where governance becomes structured practice.

---

## ⭐ **2 — Purpose**  
The Governance Practice Layer ensures:

- stable governance artifacts  
- predictable decision flows  
- clarity‑aligned communication  
- coordination‑safe interaction  
- alignment‑consistent outcomes  
- resilience‑driven pacing  

It prevents:

- collapse‑style urgency  
- authority drift  
- metaphor contamination  
- ambiguous decision logic  
- unstable governance artifacts  

This is the **operational expression** of the principle spine.

---

## ⭐ **3 — Formal Definition**  
> **The Governance Practice Layer is the structured domain where the four principles—Clarity, Coordination, Alignment, Resilience—are instantiated into charters, protocols, decision flows, communication norms, and stability practices.  
> It is non‑hierarchical, clarity‑driven, and mode‑pure.**

It is not an execution engine.  
It is not an authority structure.  
It is a **practice architecture**.

## ⭐ **5 — Protocols**

### **Protocol G‑1 — Principle Instantiation**  
All governance artifacts must instantiate:

- **Clarity**  
- **Coordination**  
- **Alignment**  
- **Resilience**  

No artifact may omit a principle.

---

### **Protocol G‑2 — Non‑Hierarchy**  
Governance artifacts must not create authority.

Formal invariant:

\[
G_{\text{artifact}} \not\rightarrow \text{authority}
\]

---

### **Protocol G‑3 — Mode Purity**  
Governance artifacts must not contain metaphor.

Formal invariant:

\[
M \not\rightarrow G
\]

---

### **Protocol G‑4 — Stability Preservation**  
Artifacts must maintain stability across revisions.

---

### **Protocol G‑5 — Transparency**  
All governance artifacts must be fully visible and interpretable.

---

## ⭐ **6 — Invariants**

### **Invariant 1 — Non‑Metaphor**  
No metaphor constructs may appear in governance artifacts.

### **Invariant 2 — Non‑Authority**  
No artifact may imply hierarchy.

### **Invariant 3 — Stability Continuity**  
Artifacts must remain stable across time.

### **Invariant 4 — Clarity Alignment**  
Artifacts must maintain clarity‑safe language.

### **Invariant 5 — Distributed Responsibility**  
No artifact may centralize responsibility.

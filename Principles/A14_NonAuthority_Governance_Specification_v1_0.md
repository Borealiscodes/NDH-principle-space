## ⭐ **2 — Purpose of A14**

A14 exists to ensure that governance:

- **does not accumulate power**  
- **does not inherit directives**  
- **does not form lineage**  
- **does not create hierarchy**  
- **does not become altitude‑root**  
- **does not become coercive**  

A14 is the invariant that says:

> **Governance must never become authority, even if it becomes effective.  
> Effectiveness is not permission for hierarchy.**

Guided Link: **Non‑Hierarchical Governance**

## ⭐ **3 — Formal Definition**

### ✔ **A14 = Non‑Authority Governance**  
A governance action must:

- **not imply authority**  
- **not create hierarchy**  
- **not generate directive inheritance**  
- **not create governance ancestry**  
- **not elevate itself into altitude‑root semantics**  
- **not accumulate structural power**  

A14 is the **non‑authority seal** that keeps governance **principle‑aligned**, not **power‑aligned**.

A14 prohibits:

- **authority drift**  
- **lineage primacy**  
- **directive inheritance**  
- **governance ancestry**  
- **altitude‑root semantics**  
- **coercive governance**

- **metaphor‑to‑power drift**  

A14 requires:

- **non‑hierarchical structure**  
- **principle‑aligned governance**  
- **translation‑layer purity**  
- **explicit non‑authority framing**  
- **bounded governance scope**

## ⭐ **5 — A14 Operational Rules**

### **5.1 — Non‑Authority Rule**  
Governance actions must never imply authority, even indirectly.

### **5.2 — Non‑Lineage Rule**  
Governance actions cannot inherit or create lineage.

### **5.3 — Non‑Directive Rule**  
Governance actions cannot become directives.

### **5.4 — Non‑Elevation Rule**  
Governance actions cannot elevate themselves into altitude‑root semantics.

### **5.5 — Non‑Coercion Rule**  
Governance actions must remain voluntary, principle‑aligned, and non‑coercive.

## ⭐ **6 — A14 Failure Modes (Prevented)**

A14 prevents:

- governance → authority  
- clarity → hierarchy  
- metaphor → directive  
- expressive geometry → governance contamination  
- recursion → governance loops  
- constellation metaphors → governance elevation  
- lineage → governance ancestry  
- ambiguity → power accumulation  

A14 is the invariant that keeps governance **safe**, **flat**, and **non‑hierarchical**.

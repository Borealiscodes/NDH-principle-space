Governance must remain sealed from NDH‑SIMULATION‑SUITE, projection geometry, and expressive manifolds.

### **3.4 Altitude Neutrality Commitment**  
Governance must not ascend into expressive altitudes or descend into runtime altitudes.

### **3.5 Multi‑Axis Compatibility Commitment**  
Governance must remain compatible with NDH’s multi‑axis architecture without referencing or activating axes.

Governance must preserve:

- intersectional structure  
- lived‑experience complexity  
- adjacency relationships  
- ethical load

### **3.2 Anti‑Flattening Commitment**  
Governance must not:

- compress  
- simplify  
- reduce  
- collapse  
- abstract away lived experience  

This prevents governance from becoming reductive or technocratic.

### **3.3 Intersectional Continuity Commitment**  
Governance must maintain:

- multi‑axis stability  
- identity continuity  
- ethical consistency  
- non‑hierarchical representation  

This ensures governance respects the lived intersection (●) without importing altitude.

### **3.4 Translation Integrity Commitment**  
The Translation Layer must:

- preserve dimensionality  
- preserve ethical load  
- preserve adjacency  
- preserve identity continuity  

This ensures governance receives a **faithful mapping**, not a flattened one.
